Anime Like Solo Leveling

Solo Leveling gives its audience the purest version of a beloved fantasy: the weakest man in the world gets a system that only he can see, and every episode he climbs. These five anime deliver different flavours of that same climb.

The show's appeal is structure as much as spectacle. The 'system' turns growth into a visible scoreboard — levels, stats, dungeons — and viewers feel every increase. The best companion anime either share that sense of measured escalation, or go the other way and parody it with total sincerity.

Each pick below is here for a specific reason, from absurd power ceilings to escalating stakes told over a decade, and each links to a BRYME title page with trailer, studio facts and viewing details.

Who this list is for

For viewers who enjoy watching a protagonist earn power on a curve they can feel — and who care more about satisfying escalation, world-building and fight design than about intricate romance or slice-of-life. All five are action-led, and several are long commitments.

Anime · 2020

Jujutsu Kaisen

The closest modern cousin: a secret system of curses and rankings, an overpowered mentor, and fights where rules matter as much as strength. Its animation escalates the way Solo Leveling's does — each major fight tries to outdo the last, and the power system keeps finding new corners.

Jujutsu Kaisen on BRYME — curses, ranks and trailer →

Anime · 2019

Demon Slayer: Kimetsu no Yaiba

If the part of Solo Leveling you loved was the feeling of a hunter training through the ranks, Demon Slayer weaponises it: breathing styles instead of spells, and a hero whose growth you track breath by breath. The animation is the current gold standard for fight choreography.

Demon Slayer on BRYME — breathing styles, explained →

Anime · 2015

One Punch Man

The joke version of the power fantasy, and that is the recommendation. Saitama is what Jinwoo is becoming: a man so far past every threat that the drama has to come from the world around him. Watch it after Solo Leveling and the comedy lands much harder.

One-Punch Man on BRYME — the max-level parody →

Anime · 2013

Attack on Titan

The escalation epic. Where Solo Leveling climbs a dungeon, this climbs the entire logic of its world — each answer rewriting the stakes of the last. If the addictive part of the 'system' for you was the slow reveal of how the world actually works, this is the deepest well on the list.

Attack on Titan on BRYME — the escalation epic →

Anime · 2002

Naruto

The origin of the modern leveling fantasy, and still the one that shows its seams most affectionately: an outcast, a forbidden power inside him, and hundreds of episodes of measurable growth. A long commitment, but the template Solo Leveling's manhwa generation grew up on.

How to watch

All five are on major streamers in most regions — Jujutsu Kaisen, Demon Slayer and Attack on Titan on Crunchyroll and Netflix in many markets, One-Punch Man split across services, Naruto widely available. Questions, quickly

What should I watch first after Solo Leveling?

Jujutsu Kaisen. It shares the modern visual language, the ranked power structure and the escalating fight design almost exactly.

Any of these short enough for a weekend?

One-Punch Man is the shortest with twelve episodes in its first season. Jujutsu Kaisen's first season is 24. The rest are much longer commitments.

Is Solo Leveling based on a manga?

On a Korean web novel and webtoon by Chugong — a manhwa, not a manga — which is part of why its system-heavy fantasy feels different from the Japanese shonen template it grew up alongside.
