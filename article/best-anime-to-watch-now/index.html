Best Anime to Watch Now (And Where to Watch Them Legally)

Seven series worth your weekend

No hype cycles, no piracy — just the anime BRYME’s catalogue actually rates, with legal where-to-watch options on every title page.

Frieren: Beyond Journey’s End — the quiet masterpiece of modern fantasy: an elf mage outliving her friends, one gentle episode at a time.

Jujutsu Kaisen — the modern shonen benchmark, with fight animation that reset industry expectations.

Chainsaw Man — grimy, funny, unhinged, and gorgeous in all the wrong ways.

DAN DA DAN — the freshest voice in years: aliens, ghosts, and two leads who argue like siblings.

One Piece — the long walk that still rewards new starters; BRYME’s page tells you where to begin legally.

Naruto — the classic that trained a generation of fans.

One Piece Film: Red — proof that a franchise film can be a proper pop concert.

How to watch, the legal way

Every anime page on BRYME links official trailers and licensed streaming platforms only — Crunchyroll, Netflix and friends. If a site offers the current season free with no rights notice, it is illegal, and usually swimming in worse ads than the show is worth. Start from the anime hub, filter by genre or year, and build your list.
