How these labels work.
🔥 Hot, 📈 Rising, 👀 Viral, 🌍 Regional/Global and 🆕 New are editorial labels on BRYME's list (content/rankings.json) — not search volume, not TikTok, not a made-up percentage score. A title is on this page because an editor put it here. Country shelves below only appear when there are enough current titles to fill them. When BRYME has real analytics, this desk can switch to engagement + growth without changing the URL.
