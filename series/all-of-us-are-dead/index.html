What All of Us Are Dead is about

All of Us Are Dead is a horror, survival and thriller series directed by Kim Nam-su and Lee Jae-gyu and released in 2022. It comes from Korea. The cast includes Park Ji-hu, Yoon Chan-young, Cho Yi-hyun, Lomon.
