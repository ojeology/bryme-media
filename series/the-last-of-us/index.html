Quick summary

HBO's adaptation of the beloved video game. Joel, a broken smuggler, must escort Ellie, a teenage girl immune to the fungal pandemic, across a ruined America. Craig Mazin (Chernobyl) and the game's creator Neil Druckmann co-wrote it.

What The Last of Us is about

The Last of Us is a survival, drama and horror series directed by Craig Mazin, Kantemir Balagov and Neil Druckmann and released in 2023. It comes from United States. The cast includes Pedro Pascal, Bella Ramsey, Gabriel Luna, Isabela Merced.
