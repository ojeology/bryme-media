Quick summary

Hwang Dong-hyuk’s Squid Game puts desperate contestants into playground rules with lethal stakes. It is Netflix’s giant hit for a reason: the games are easy to explain and hard to sit through. BRYME also has a Season 1 piece and an Alice in Borderland comparison if you want the argument, not just the recap.

What Squid Game is about

Gi-hun and the others are not action heroes. They are people who ran out of ordinary exits. The show uses bright sets so the violence reads as policy, not as grit cosplay.

If you are here from a status video, start with Season 1 and then read BRYME’s comparison with Alice in Borderland. They scratch a similar itch. They do not make the same point.
