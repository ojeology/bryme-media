What Black Mirror is about

Black Mirror is a sci-fi, thriller and drama series directed by John Hillcoat, Colm McCarthy, Euros Lyn, Brian Welsh, Otto Bathurst, Carl Tibbetts, James Hawes, Tim Van Patten, Owen Harris, Bryn Higgins, David Slade, Joe Wright, Jakob Verbruggen, James Watkins, Dan Trachtenberg, Jodie Foster and Toby Haynes and released in 2011. It comes from United Kingdom. The series runs about 64 minutes.
