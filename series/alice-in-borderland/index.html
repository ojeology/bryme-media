Alice in Borderland

  • Year 2020
  • Country Japan
  • Director Shinsuke Sato
  • Starring Kento Yamazaki, Tao Tsuchiya, Nijirō Murakami, Ayaka Miyoshi
  • Genre Survival, Thriller, Sci-Fi

Based on Haro Aso's manga and directed across its run by Shinsuke Sato, Alice in Borderland introduces Arisu — a bored gamer with no particular ambition — and drops him, along with two friends, into a version of Tokyo that has been emptied of everyone else. The city still works. The people are gone. Every crossing at Shibuya is silent.

The premise arrives quickly: to stay alive in this Tokyo you must win games, and to win you must first work out what game you are actually playing. Cards set the difficulty, new arrivals complicate the groups, and the borderland turns out to have rules — and managers — of its own.

Why it is worth your evening

It is one of the cleanest examples of a show built on game design rather than monsters: every episode hands the audience the same information the characters have and lets you do the maths alongside them. The performances — Kento Yamazaki's cautious Arisu against Tao Tsuchiya's practical Usagi — carry the quieter stretches, and the visual idea of a crowded city drained of people remains the show's signature.

How to watch it

A Netflix original in most regions. The first two seasons tell a complete arc; the franchise has continued since, so check your regional catalogue for the current season list. Subtitles are the standard way to watch, with dubs available in several languages.
