What The Host is about

The Host is a horror film directed by Bong Joon-ho and released in 2006. It comes from South Korea, spoken in Korean. The cast includes Song Kang-ho, Byun Hee-bong, Park Hae-il, Bae Doona. The film runs about 119 minutes.
