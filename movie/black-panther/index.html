★ 8/10

BRYME editorial opinion — not IMDb, Rotten Tomatoes, or a live audience score.

It is the Marvel film that still works if you do not care about the Infinity Stone chart.

Quick summary

Ryan Coogler’s Black Panther is a superhero film that keeps asking a policy question: does the most advanced nation on Earth stay hidden, or does it step out? Chadwick Boseman’s T’Challa inherits a throne and an argument. BRYME lists it as superhero cinema that is also a country.

What Black Panther is about

The set pieces are Marvel’s. The interesting film is the debate between isolation and intervention, played through family and a rival who has a point. Killmonger is not a twist villain you forget.

If you are here for the suit, you will get the suit. If you are building a list that includes Creed and Sinners, you are following Coogler, not the MCU calendar.

Why you might like it

It is the Marvel film that still works if you do not care about the Infinity Stone chart.

Coogler’s other films on BRYME are the better ‘if you liked this’ path than another Avengers entry.

Wakanda Forever is the sequel. It is a different grief.
