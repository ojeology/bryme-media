Quick summary

The prequel to Fury Road, told over fifteen years. Young Furiosa is taken from her home, survives warlords, and slowly becomes the warrior who will drive the War Rig to freedom. Anya Taylor-Joy takes over the role.

What Furiosa: A Mad Max Saga is about

Furiosa: A Mad Max Saga is an action film directed by George Miller and released in 2024. It comes from United States, Australia. The cast includes Anya Taylor-Joy, Chris Hemsworth, Tom Burke, Alyla Browne. The film runs about 148 minutes.
