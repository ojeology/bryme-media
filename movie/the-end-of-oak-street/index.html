Quick summary

David Robert Mitchell's The End of Oak Street follows the Platt family after their suburban block is ripped out of the present. Warner Bros. opened it in North America on 14 August 2026, the same weekend as PAW Patrol: The Dino Movie. Anne Hathaway and Ewan McGregor play the parents.

What The End of Oak Street is about

The official site, endofoakstreet.com, describes a Bad Robot production of a David Robert Mitchell film, distributed by Warner Bros., with an IMAX date. The YouTube trailer on this page is from Warner Bros. India, an official regional distributor channel, published 22 July 2026 — not a fan reupload.

Mitchell previously made It Follows. That is context for tone, not a promise this film is the same machine.
