Quick summary

Toy Story 5 puts Woody, Buzz and Bonnie's room up against Lilypad, a tablet with ideas about what a child needs. Andrew Stanton directs for Pixar. It opened in US theatres on 19 June 2026 and, by mid-August, was still on the North American chart.

What Toy Story 5 is about

Pixar's official trailer, published 19 February 2026, is the clip here. The billed voice names on that upload include Tom Hanks, Tim Allen, Joan Cusack and Greta Lee as Lilypad.

Disney's own film page also dated a digital release for 18 August 2026 and Blu-ray for 22 September. That is a studio timetable, not a Netflix listing, and not a BRYME shop.
