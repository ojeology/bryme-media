What The Good, the Bad and the Ugly is about

The Good, the Bad and the Ugly is a western film directed by Sergio Leone and released in 1966. It comes from Italy. The cast includes Clint Eastwood, Eli Wallach, Lee Van Cleef, Aldo Giuffrè. The film runs about 161 minutes.
