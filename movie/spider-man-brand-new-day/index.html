🔥 Why it's trending

On 18 August 2026 The Numbers had Brand New Day at number one at the North American box office, with a domestic total around $804 million. Deadline reported the same week that it had crossed $2 billion worldwide in 17 days, the second-fastest film to that mark after Avengers: Endgame.

That is why it sits at the top of BRYME's current movie list. Avengers: Doomsday and Dune: Part Three remain in the catalogue for December. They are not pretending to be in theatres this week.

Quick summary

Destin Daniel Cretton's Spider-Man: Brand New Day picks up four years after No Way Home. Peter is an adult, living with the spell that erased him, and still putting the suit on in a New York that does not know his name. BRYME is listing it now because it is the film at the top of the August 2026 box office — not because a December Avengers date is closer on a calendar.

What Spider-Man: Brand New Day is about

The official trailer, published on the Spider-Man YouTube channel on 18 March 2026, states the premise in Peter's own voice: something bad was coming, the only way to stop it was to make everyone forget him, and he is still Spider-Man. Credits on that upload name Destin Daniel Cretton as director, Chris McKenna and Erik Sommers as writers, and a billed cast of Tom Holland, Zendaya, Sadie Sink, Jacob Batalon, Jon Bernthal, Tramell Tillman, Michael Mando and Mark Ruffalo.

This page is a discovery note, not a recap of the film and not a claim that BRYME sat the runtime. The trailer is Sony/Marvel marketing. It is not a plot guarantee.
