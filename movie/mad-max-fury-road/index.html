Quick summary

George Miller return to the wasteland after thirty years. Furiosa escapes the tyrant Immortan Joe with his five wives, and Max becomes an unwilling ally in a two-hour chase that never lets go.

What Mad Max: Fury Road is about

Mad Max: Fury Road is an action film directed by George Miller and released in 2015. It comes from Australia, United States. The cast includes Charlize Theron, Tom Hardy, Nicholas Hoult, Hugh Keays-Byrne. The film runs about 120 minutes.
