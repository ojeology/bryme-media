What Seven Samurai is about

Seven Samurai is an action film directed by Akira Kurosawa and released in 1954. It comes from Japan, spoken in Japanese. The cast includes Toshiro Mifune, Takashi Shimura, Keiko Tsushima, Isao Kimura. The film runs about 207 minutes.
