What Once Upon a Time in the West is about

Once Upon a Time in the West is a western film directed by Sergio Leone and released in 1968. It comes from Italy. The cast includes Claudia Cardinale, Henry Fonda, Jason Robards, Charles Bronson. The film runs about 140 minutes.
