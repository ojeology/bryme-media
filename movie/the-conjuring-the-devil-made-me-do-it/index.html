Quick summary

The third Conjuring film — and the first true story that goes to court. Ed and Lorraine Warren investigate the Arne Johnson case: the first US murder trial where demonic possession was the legal defense. A possession film that becomes a detective story.

What The Conjuring: The Devil Made Me Do It is about

The Conjuring: The Devil Made Me Do It is a horror film directed by Michael Chaves and released in 2021. It comes from United States. The cast includes Vera Farmiga, Patrick Wilson, Ruairi O'Connor, Sarah Catherine Hook. The film runs about 112 minutes.

Notes

  • The film is based on the real 1981 'Devil Made Me Do It' murder case — the first time possession was used as a legal defense in the US.
  • Patrick Wilson and Vera Farmiga are the only actors to appear in every Conjuring film.
  • The 'conjuring universe' now spans 8+ films and billions in box office.
  • The real Ed Warren was a demonologist and author; the film tones down his controversial reputation.
