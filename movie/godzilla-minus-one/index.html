Quick summary

Set in postwar Japan, a kamikaze pilot returns home to a destroyed country and faces a new terror rising from the sea. It became the first Japanese Godzilla film to win an Oscar.

What Godzilla Minus One is about

Godzilla Minus One is an action film directed by Takashi Yamazaki and released in 2023. It comes from Japan, spoken in Japanese. The cast includes Ryunosuke Kamiki, Minami Hamabe, Yuki Yamada, Munetaka Aoki. The film runs about 125 minutes.

Notes

  • It won the Oscar for Best Visual Effects on a budget of about 15 million dollars.
  • The film is set in 1945-1947, right after World War Two.
  • The director also wrote and edited the film and handled much of the VFX himself.
  • The monster design returns to the original 1954 silhouette.
