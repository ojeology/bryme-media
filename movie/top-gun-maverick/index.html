Quick summary

Tom Cruise returns as Pete Maverick Mitchell, now a test pilot forced to train a new generation for an impossible mission. Shot with real jets and real G-forces, it is the rare legacy sequel that outflies the original.

Notes

  • The actors really flew in F-18s and endured up to 7.5 Gs during filming.
  • There is no CGI for the flight scenes, only practical aerial cinematography.
  • Maverick is the only Tom Cruise film where he actually plays a father figure to his rival son.
  • The final canyon run was filmed in real locations, not a set.
