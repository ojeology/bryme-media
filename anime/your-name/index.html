Quick summary

Makoto Shinkai's masterpiece. A rural girl and a Tokyo boy begin swapping bodies — and then, suddenly, it stops. A story about time, distance, and the red thread of fate, with some of the most beautiful animation ever drawn.

What Your Name is about

Your Name is a romance, drama and fantasy anime directed by Makoto Shinkai and released in 2016. It comes from Japan, spoken in Japanese. The cast includes Ryunosuke Kamiki, Mone Kamishiraishi. The anime runs about 107 minutes.

Notes

  • It became the highest-grossing anime film ever at release, beating Spirited Away's record.
  • Director Makoto Shinkai based the town scenes on real locations in Hida, Japan.
  • The film's train-crossing scene was drawn from Shinkai's own childhood memory.
  • It took over a year to fully hand-draw the comet sequences.
