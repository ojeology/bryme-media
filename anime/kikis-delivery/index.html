What Kiki's Delivery Service is about

Kiki's Delivery Service is a family, fantasy and slice of life anime directed by Hayao Miyazaki and released in 1989. It comes from Japan. The cast includes Minami Takayama, Rei Sakuma, Kappei Yamaguchi, Keiko Toda. The anime runs about 103 minutes.
