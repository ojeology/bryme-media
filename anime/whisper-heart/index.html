What Whisper of the Heart is about

Whisper of the Heart is a romance, slice of life and drama anime directed by Yoshifumi Kondō and released in 1995. It comes from Japan. The cast includes Yōko Honna, Issey Takahashi, Takashi Tachibana (journalist), Shigeru Muroi. The anime runs about 111 minutes.
