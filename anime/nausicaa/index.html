What Nausicaä of the Valley of the Wind is about

Nausicaä of the Valley of the Wind is an adventure, fantasy and sci-fi anime directed by Hayao Miyazaki and released in 1984. It comes from Japan. The cast includes Sumi Shimamoto, Gorō Naya, Yōji Matsuda, Yoshiko Sakakibara. The anime runs about 117 minutes.
