What Porco Rosso is about

Porco Rosso is an adventure, comedy and historical anime directed by Hayao Miyazaki and released in 1992. It comes from Japan. The cast includes Shūichirō Moriyama, Tokiko Kato, Akemi Okamura, Akio Otsuka. The anime runs about 92 minutes.
