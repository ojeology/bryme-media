Solo Leveling

  • Year 2024
  • Country Japan
  • Genre Action, Fantasy, Adventure
Solo Leveling — official trailerclick to load the player

Privacy-enhanced player, loaded only on click.

Sung Jinwoo is the weakest hunter alive — a man with a minimum-wage body in a world where gate-born monsters reward the strong. Then a lethal dungeon hands him a private system only he can see: quests, levels, stats, and a daily workout notification that mocks him into survival. By the end of its first arc, the premise is set and there is no going back.

Produced by A-1 Pictures and adapted from Chugong's Korean web novel and webtoon, the series is part power fantasy, part video-game logic made literal. The 'system' is not a metaphor here; it is a character, and its prompts frame every episode.

Why it is worth your evening

What makes the show work is that it treats leveling as narrative structure, not decoration. Viewers always know Jinwoo's curve — what he was, what he now is — and the fights escalate on that visible scoreboard. It is also a clean gateway between anime and Korean webtoon storytelling: same animation craftsmanship as shonen, different instincts about how fast a story should climb.

How to watch it

Streaming on Crunchyroll and Netflix in most regions, with a second season continuing the story. Japanese anime adaptation of material originally written in Korean — the manhwa is widely translated, including officially, if you want to read ahead.
