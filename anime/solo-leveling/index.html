Quick summary

Solo Leveling follows Sung Jinwoo, an E-Rank hunter who survives a double dungeon and wakes up with a leveling system only he can see. It is power-fantasy anime done clean: gates, ranks, and a climb that is supposed to feel unfair in his favour. BRYME has more than one Solo Leveling piece if you want ‘what to watch next’ rather than another recap.

What Solo Leveling is about

The pleasure is the gap. Jinwoo starts disposable. The system makes him a secret. The adaptation from Chugong’s web novel and manhwa keeps that loop: fight, level, hide the truth, fight something worse.

If you bounced off talky anime, this one is simpler. If you want character ensembles like Hunter × Hunter, this is a different machine. BRYME’s comparison article is there for that fork.
