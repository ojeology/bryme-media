Ligue 1 Transfers 2026/27

Last updated: 13 August 2026

18 clubs, 12 with a new head coach this summer — a record for Ligue 1, beating the previous high of 9 (matched in 2002/03, 2009/10 and 2021/22). Metz and Nantes were relegated to Ligue 2; Troyes and Le Mans came up in their place. The transfer window opened 15 June 2026.

Transfer information changes frequently. This list is updated as new information becomes available and may not include breaking or unannounced developments.
