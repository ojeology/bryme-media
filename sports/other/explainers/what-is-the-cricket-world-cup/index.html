What is the Cricket World Cup?

Evergreen • Updated 02 Oct 2026 • 3 min read
Answer: The ODI Cricket World Cup is 10 teams over 47 days every 4 years — 10 play a single round-robin 9 games each, top 4 to semis, single elimination to the final over 100 overs per day, not 20, with the 2023 India 10-0 group then 12-man heartbreak the evergreen.

Explained properly

The ODI World Cup is 10 teams in 2023 and 2027 — 10 play single round-robin 9 each over 45 days, then 1st vs 4th and 2nd vs 3rd semis single game over 100 overs (50 per side), final over 100 overs at Ahmedabad's 132,000 — the 10 not 32 makes the 9-group the league, not the group, which is why Afghanistan's 4 wins in 2023 still meant 6th and out, while 4 in football's 3-group can mean knockout — the 9 vs 3 defines the footprint.

The qualifier explains India 2023's depth. The World Cup Qualifier with 10 teams for the last 2 spots — 2 of 32 Associates plus Full Members — Sri Lanka and Netherlands qualified via Zimbabwe 2023 for the 10, so the 10 are 8 auto plus 2 via qualifier, which is why West Indies missed the 2023 World Cup for the first time — the 10, not 14 as in 2015, needs 2 via qualifier, not just ranking, so the 2015 14-team left no qualifier bottleneck but the 10-team did.

The 50 overs explains the day. One innings 50 overs of 6 balls — 300 balls per innings, 600 per day, 8 hours including lunch, with powerplay 10 overs with 2 fielders outside circle, 30 overs with 4 outside, 10 with 5 outside — the powerplay's 10-30-10 sets the field that decides 320 par, not the 200 of T20's 20 overs, which is why the ODI 400 is rare and 320 is par, not 170 as in T20 or 450 as in Test's 90 overs per day.

The evergreen is 2019 tie. England vs New Zealand tie 241-241 in the final, super over tie 15-15, boundary countback gave England the win on 26-17 — the law since changed to successive super overs until win, no countback — the 2019 26-17 boundary is the only World Cup won on boundaries, not runs, which is why the ODI laws now have two super overs, not one plus boundaries, like football's away goals now removed.

The T20 World Cup is 20 teams now 2024 — 20 teams, 4 groups of 5, top 2 to Super 8, then semis — the ODI is 10 over 47 days, T20 20 over 29 days, so the ODI's single round-robin 9 is the endurance league, T20's 5-group is the sprint, which is why the IPL's 74 games feed the T20 World Cup more than the ODI — the 50 vs 20 splits the preparation.

Picture this: Ahmedabad 2023: 10 teams 9 each — India 9-0 group, South Africa 7-2, Australia 7-2, New Zealand 5-4 semis — India vs New Zealand semi, South Africa vs Australia semi, Australia beat India in the final over 100 overs — 45 days round-robin plus 3 knockouts, powerplay 10 with 2 outside, 400 rare 320 par — the 10-team ODI vs 2019's tied super over 15-15 26-17 boundaries that law now removes.

Myths vs reality

“Cricket World Cup is T20.”
Reality: ODI 50-over 10 teams over 47 days vs T20 20 teams over 29 days — 10 single round-robin 9 each, top 4 semis over 100 overs per day.
“All qualify automatically.”
Reality: 10 via 8 auto plus 2 from 10-team qualifier — West Indies missed 2023 as 2-2 holder needs 3-2, first time out.
“Tie is shared.”
Reality: Super over — 2019 tie 241-241 plus super 15-15 was England on 26-17 boundaries, now successive super overs, not countback.

FAQ

How many teams at ODI Cricket World Cup?

10 — single round-robin 9 each over 45 days, top 4 to semis, 100 overs per day over 8 hours.

How long is an ODI?

50 overs per side, 6 balls per over, 300 balls per innings, powerplay 10-30-10 with 2-4-5 fielders outside circle.

What was 2019 final tie?

England 241-241 tie, super over 15-15 tie, England won on 26-17 boundaries — law now successive super overs, not boundaries.
