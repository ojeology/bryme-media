What are the Davis Cup and Billie Jean King Cup?

Evergreen • Updated 02 Oct 2026 • 3 min read
Answer: The Davis Cup (men) and Billie Jean King Cup (women) are the national-team World Cups of tennis — 16 teams in the Finals each November, with ties of 2 singles and 1 doubles over 1 day, played home-or-away in the qualifiers with 128+ nations in the pyramid, not the 128-player Slam draw.

Explained properly

The Davis Cup is nations, not players — Canada vs Italy is 3 matches: 2 singles and 1 doubles over one day, best of 3 ties, with singles 4 of 96 and doubles 1 of 48 plus doubles team, so the World No 1 can lose if his No 2 loses both singles, which is why Sinner can be 1 but Italy without Sinner can still lose the qualifier tie at home — the 2+1 makes the 2nd singles the tie, not the star.

The pyramid widens it. Qualifiers February are 24 ties home-or-away knockout, winners to September Finals group of 16 in 4 groups of 4 over 6 days, then 8-team knockout November in Malaga or Seville — the Billie Jean King Cup mirrored with 12 not 16 — so the 128 nations play home ties in zone groups September-February before the 16-team Finals, which is why tennis has a February home Davis Cup week with 5,000 crowds not on TV but the November Finals on centre court with Hawkeye.

The surface choice decides. The home nation chooses surface — Spain chooses clay in Cordoba, USA chooses hard in New York, Great Britain indoor hard in Glasgow — so the tie's 2+1 can be loaded for the host's clay specialist over the visitor's hard hitter, which is why the Davis Cup upset is surface, not ranking — Argentina on clay beat USA on clay in 2008 with Nalbandian beating Roddick on clay, not hard.

The evergreen myth is Davis Cup is doubles only. It is 2 singles plus doubles — the 3rd singles matters if split 1-1 after day 1 would have been, but since 2019 the format is 2 singles plus doubles over one day, so a 2-0 lead wins before doubles, which is why the ticket says 2 singles first then doubles, not doubles first, and why the fan buys 1 day not 3.

The ATP and WTA do not rank Davis Cup points — zero since 2016, so the tie is pride plus $15m prize pool not ranking, which is why top 10 can skip the February qualifier and still be 1 seed at March Masters — the ranking and the Cup are separate, like AFCON's group vs league points.

Picture this: February qualifier: Italy vs Netherlands in Bologna indoor hard — Italy without Sinner lose singles 1 and doubles but win singles 2, 1-2 loses the tie — November Finals in Malaga Group A Italy beat Chile 2-1 then quarters vs Netherlands 2-0, same 2+1 — the 3-match tie with 16-team Finals November after 24 February home-or-away qualifiers with 128 nations descending to 16, surface chosen by host.

Myths vs reality

“Davis Cup is doubles.”
Reality: 2 singles plus 1 doubles over 1 day — 2-0 ends before doubles, so the 2nd singles decides more ties than the doubles.
“It is all season.”
Reality: February 24 qualifiers home-or-away, September 4x4 Finals group, November 8-team knockout — the 6 days not the 12-month league.
“Ranking points count.”
Reality: Zero since 2016 — $15m prize not ATP points, so the No 1 can skip February and still be 1 in March.

FAQ

What is the Davis Cup?

Men's tennis World Cup — 16 teams in Finals November, ties 2 singles plus 1 doubles over 1 day, 24 February home-or-away qualifiers feeding the 16.

What is the Billie Jean King Cup?

Women's equivalent — 12 Finals in November, same 2+1 tie format over 1 day, named after the pioneer.

Does Davis Cup give ranking points?

No since 2016 — prize and pride, not ATP points, so top 10 can skip qualifiers.
