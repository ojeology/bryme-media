How does swimming at the Olympics work?

Evergreen • Updated 02 Oct 2026 • 3 min read
Answer: Olympic swimming is 35 events — 17 per gender plus 1 mixed medley relay — per stroke over 50m to 1500m in a 50m long-course pool, with heats, semis and a final where 0.01 decides, 0.00 is a touchpad, and the 8-lane final has no best-thirds — 16.00 makes the final, 16.01 goes home, which is why 0.01 is the evergreen margin.

Explained properly

The pool explains the time. The long course is 50m, 8 lanes, 2.5m per lane, with touchpads that stop on 0.01 and a false start beep for the dive — the short course 25m is 2 seconds faster per 100m because of more turns and push-offs, so Olympic records are long course only, which is why the 100m free long course is 46.80 and short course 44.84 for the same swimmer — the turns, not the water, decide.

The strokes explain the 35. Freestyle 50, 100, 200, 400, 800, 1500, backstroke 100 and 200, breaststroke 100 and 200, butterfly 100 and 200, individual medley 200 and 400, plus relays 4x100 free, 4x200 free, 4x100 medley, plus the mixed 4x100 medley — the medley relay order is back, breast, fly, free so the back starts in the water, which is why the mixed medley's order can be man-woman-man-woman in any stroke but the back must start.

Rounds explain the week. The 100m has heats 64, semis 16, final 8 over two days — 64 in the morning heats, 16 in the evening semis, 8 in the next evening final — so a swimmer who makes the final swims 3 times, which is why the heats are at 80% to save the 0.01 for the final, and a 16th on 48.02 who ties for 16th swims off at 21:00 with one lane — the swim-off is the evergreen 0.01 decider that football has no equivalent for.

Why 0.01 is not photo is the pad. The touchpad is 2m by 1m at the end, pressed by the hand, not the head, at 0.01, so a finish with a fingertip vs a palm can be 0.02 — the pad, not the eye, decides, and the Omega quantum timer in 2024 resolved to 0.001 for the judges but displayed to 0.01, so a 0.001 tie is still 0.01 on the board and the swim-off decides, not the thousandth.

The suit explains the 2009-10 records. The polyurethane suit that covered the body added buoyancy and was banned in 2010, so the 2008-09 records in the suit still stand in some events — the 100m free suit record of 47.05 was beaten only in 2023 by 46.80, 15 years later, which is why the evergreen question is which records are suit-era and which are post-suit textile, and why the 1500m freestyle has not been broken since 2023.

Relays explain the team. The 4x100 free relay is 4x100 per nation, 8 nations in the final, with relay takeovers measured at 0.00 — the next swimmer can dive when the incoming touches, so a -0.10 takeover is a disqualification, which is why the relay's 0.00 exchange is the football offside line of swimming.

Picture this: Paris 2024 final night: Men's 100m free final — 8 lanes, 46.80 winning, 2nd 47.04 by 0.24, women's 100m breast — 1:04.40, heats 1:06.00, semis 1:05.00, final 1:04.40 — one touch on the pad at 0.01 decides 16th vs 17th in the heats, and the 4x100 medley relay final has the backstroker start in the water at 0.00 takeover, no best-thirds, 0.01 on the pad is the final, not the table.

Myths vs reality

“Swimming is just fast laps.”
Reality: 35 events — 6 frees 50-1500, 4 strokes x2 distances, 2 medleys, 3 relays plus mixed medley — heats, semis, final where 0.01 on the pad, not points, decides.
“Short course is the same.”
Reality: 25m vs 50m — short course is 2 seconds faster per 100m because of more turns and push-offs, so Olympic records are long course only, 46.80 vs 44.84 for 100m free.
“A tie can share gold.”
Reality: If 0.01 ties, the tie is displayed as the same time but the judges' 0.001 may split it — a swim-off decides 16th vs 17th, and a final tie can share if 0.01 still ties.

FAQ

How many swimming events at the Olympics?

35 — 17 per gender plus 1 mixed medley relay, per stroke 50-1500m in a 50m pool, heats, semis, final where 0.01 on the touchpad decides.

Why is short course faster?

25m has more turns and push-offs per 100m than 50m — about 2 seconds per 100m faster, so Olympic 50m records are long course only.

What is a swim-off?

A tied 16th on 0.01 swims again at 21:00 for the 16th final spot — the 0.01 tie-break that football has no equivalent for.
