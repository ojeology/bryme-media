How does rugby league differ from union?

Evergreen • Updated 02 Oct 2026 • 3 min read
Answer: Rugby league is 13-a-side, 80 minutes, 6 tackles then handover, no rucks, no lineouts, 4-point try plus 2 conversion plus 2 penalty and 1 drop goal, with NRL 17 teams and Super League 12, with State of Origin 3-game Queensland vs NSW, with league's 0-6 tackle count not union's ruck contest, with 10m onside not union's gate.

Explained properly

League 13 vs union 15 is 2 fewer — league removes 2 flankers, so 6 forwards plus 7 backs =13 vs union 8 forwards plus 7 backs =15 — league 6 tackles then handover at 0-6 count, union ruck contest after tackle with release plus gate — league play-the-ball vs union ruck — the 6 tackles with 0-6 call by referee with 10m defensive line vs union's offside line hindmost foot — the league 6-tackle handover not union's ruck contest with tackler roll away plus gate plus jackal — league's 4-point try then 2 conversion plus 2 penalty plus 1 drop goal vs union 5 plus 2 plus 3 plus 3 — league 4 vs union 5 with 1 point gap plus conversion 2 both not 5 — the 4 vs 5 shows league's 8-0 vs union's 7-3 in NRL's 8 tries 32 vs union's 3 tries 21.

The NRL vs Super League myth: NRL 17 teams 24 rounds home-and-away plus magic Round plus finals 8 of 17 top 8 playoffs double elimination with 2 weeks finals then grand final vs Super League 12 teams 27 rounds 12 vs 12 home-and-away plus Magic plus 6 playoffs — State of Origin 3-game June-July Queensland Maroons vs NSW Blues 52,000 Suncorp with Origin 3-game with 40m kick return vs NRL 17's 35,000 average — league's Origin 3-game not union's 6 Nations 6-team 5-round 4 points plus bonus not 6-team 5 with try bonus — the 13 vs 15 with 6 tackles shows league's 0-6 then handover 6th tackle kick not union's ruck's 15 vs 15 with 80 minutes 2x40 both — the league's NRL grand final October at Accor 82,500 vs Super League grand final October at Old Trafford 60,000 with 13 vs 15 not 15's Twickenham 82,000.

Picture this: Origin 2023: Queensland 2-1 over NSW Game3 24-10 at Suncorp 52,500 — NRL Panthers beat Broncos 26-24 grand final October Accor 82,500 — league 13 with 6 tackles 0-6 handover 10m line vs union 15 with ruck 80 minutes 4-point try 2 conversion 2 penalty 1 drop goal — the 13 vs 15 with 6 tackles then handover vs ruck contest with gate plus jackal — Super League 12 Wigan vs Catalans 10-2 final Old Trafford 60,000.

Myths vs reality

“League and union same.”
Reality: League 13 with 6 tackles 0-6 then handover 10m vs union 15 with ruck contest after tackle with release gate — 4-point try vs 5.
“League has rucks.”
Reality: No ruck or lineout — play-the-ball 6 tackles not ruck contest with tackler roll away gate — league 10m line vs union hindmost foot.
“Origin is World Cup.”
Reality: Origin 3-game June-July Queensland vs NSW 52,500 Suncorp not 20-team World Cup 4 pools 5 — club Origin 3 vs Test 20 over 7 weeks.

FAQ

Difference union vs league?

Union 15 with ruck 5-point try, league 13 with 6 tackles 0-6 handover 4-point try — no lineout or ruck in league.

How many tackles league?

6 tackles 0-6 then handover or kick — 10m defensive line not hindmost foot gate.

What is State of Origin?

3-game Queensland vs NSW June-July Suncorp 52,500 — not 20-team World Cup 4 pools 5.
