What is the EuroLeague in basketball?

Evergreen • Updated 02 Oct 2026 • 3 min read
Answer: The EuroLeague is basketball's Champions League — 18 teams, double round-robin 34 games October-April, top 6 direct to playoffs plus 7-10 Play-In for 7th and 8th, then best-of-5 quarters and a single Final Four in May at a neutral, with no relegation and licences, not promotion.

Explained properly

The EuroLeague is 18 teams — 18 licences plus wild cards, not 18 national champions — so Real Madrid, Barcelona, Olympiacos, Fenerbahce, Panathinaikos and Monaco are in every year on 10-year licences, while the domestic champions of Serbia or Lithuania join via wild card or EuroCup win, which is why the EuroLeague is not the domestic league's top 2 but the 18 licences, and why Partizan can be in without winning the ABA and why London may enter on a wild card.

The 34-game league explains the table. Double round-robin 34 games home and away October-April, then top 6 directly to the best-of-5 quarterfinals — 1 vs 8, 2 vs 7, 3 vs 6, 4 vs 5 in a 2-2-1 format, first to 3 wins, then the Final Four is two semis and a final over one weekend at a neutral — Berlin 2024, Kaunas 2023 — so the 34-game table decides the 8 and the Final Four decides the champion in 2 games, not a 7-game series like the NBA.

Why no relegation is the licence. The 18 licences are 10-year commercial deals, so a 17th on 8 wins in April stays, while a domestic champion on 25 wins in its league does not enter unless wild-carded, which is why the EuroLeague is closed like the NBA, not open like the Champions League's 36, and why the EuroCup — the second tier with 20 teams — is the only promotion path via its winner's wild card.

The Final Four is single elimination. Two semis on Friday, final on Sunday — one bad half and the 34-game top seed can lose to the 4th, as Real Madrid did vs Panathinaikos in 2024 despite 1st in the league, which is why the EuroLeague's 34 sets the 8, but the neutral Final Four is the variance that the NBA's 7-game quarters avoid — the EuroLeague is football's Final Four, not basketball's 7-game, for that reason.

The evergreen myth is the NBA vs EuroLeague gap. EuroLeague teams beat NBA teams in preseason friendlies but lose by 15 in the NBA regular because the NBA three is a metre farther and the game 8 minutes longer, but the EuroLeague's 24-second shot clock and goaltending rules are the same as FIBA, so the EuroLeague is the FIBA pro, not the NBA minor, which is why a EuroLeague MVP like Vezenkov can be an NBA rotation, not a star.

Picture this: Regular season 34: Real Madrid 27-7 first, Olympiacos 22-12 fourth, Panathinaikos 23-11 third, then quarters best of 5 — Real beat Baskonia 3-0, Olympiacos beat Barcelona 3-2, then Final Four in Berlin — Olympiacos beat Real in the semi, Panathinaikos beat Fener in the other, Panathinaikos beat Real 95-80 in the final — 34 for seeding, 2 for the title, the licence not promotion decides the 18.

Myths vs reality

“The EuroLeague is domestic champions.”
Reality: 18 licences (10-year) plus wild cards — Real, Barca and Olympiacos are in yearly, a national champion needs a wild card or EuroCup win, not just the title.
“The EuroLeague is open like football.”
Reality: Closed like the NBA — no relegation, 34 double round-robin plus best-of-5 quarters and a single Final Four neutral, not a 36-team open league.
“The best regular season wins.”
Reality: Top 6 direct plus 7-10 Play-In, then Final Four single semis and final — the 1st after 34 can lose one half and be out, as Real did in 2024.

FAQ

How many teams in the EuroLeague?

18 — double round-robin 34 games Oct-April, top 6 direct plus 7-10 Play-In, best-of-5 quarters then single Final Four neutral.

Is there relegation?

No, 10-year licences — 17th stays, domestic champions need a wild card or EuroCup win to enter.

Where is the Final Four?

Neutral over one weekend in May — Berlin 2024, Kaunas 2023 — two semis Friday, final Sunday.
