What is the Basketball Africa League (BAL)?

Evergreen • Updated 02 Oct 2026 • 3 min read
Answer: The Basketball Africa League is Africa's 12-team Champions League for clubs, launched by the NBA and FIBA in 2021 — 12 champions play a 3-conference group stage in Dakar, Cairo and Kigali, then 8 to the playoffs in Kigali, 40-minute FIBA games, winner qualifies for the FIBA Intercontinental Cup, prize $400k plus $1m NBA Africa development.

Explained properly

The BAL is 12 clubs, not countries — 12 national champions qualify via their leagues or the Road to BAL qualifier with 6 groups, so Petro de Luanda, Al Ahly, AS Douanes and Rwanda Energy Group enter as champions, not Nigeria or Senegal, which is why the BAL is the club, not the national team, and why the Nigerian champion Rivers Hoopers can be in while the Nigerian national team watches in the stands.

The format is 3 conferences of 4 in March-April — Nile, Sahara and Kalahari — each plays 3 round-robin games in one host city (Dakar, Cairo, Kigali), then ranking 1-8 go to Kigali in May for single-elimination quarters, semis and final. The 12 to 8 means 4 go home after the group, so the 3 games in the conference decide everything with no second chance like the EuroLeague's 34 games.

Why NBA not FIBA matters is the money. The NBA owns 50% and operates the commercial, FIBA the sporting — the $1m bonus is NBA Africa's fund for academies, not prize, which is why the BAL final is in Kigali's 10,000 BK Arena built for it, with NBA-style timeouts and replays, not the FIBA arena's bare broadcast, and why the MVP gets a G-League showcase, not just the trophy.

The FIBA rules make it familiar. The BAL plays FIBA 4x10, not NBA 4x12, with the FIBA trapezoid not NBA rectangle, and the goaltending and travelling calls are FIBA, which is why an NBA prospect like Victor Wembanyama's 2023 Paris scouting was at FIBA, not BAL, but BAL's best like Nuni Omot still needs FIBA reads to win.

The Intercontinental Cup is the evergreen carrot. The BAL winner qualifies for the September Intercontinental Cup vs the EuroLeague, NBA G-League and Asian champions — the only path for an African club to play a EuroLeague club competitively, which is why Al Ahly's 2023 BAL win over AS Douanes was not just $400k but the ticket to Singapore and the 12-team global table Africa had never been on before 2021.

Picture this: Kigali May: Al Ahly Cairo beat AS Douanes Dakar 80-65 in the 2023 final after topping the Nile Conference 3-0 — 4 of 12 to Kigali, 8-team single knockout, 40 minutes FIBA, not 48 NBA, with Petro de Luanda 3rd after the Road to BAL — the 12-club Africa Champions League that FIBA ran before but the NBA branded and funded in 2021.

Myths vs reality

“The BAL is for national teams.”
Reality: 12 champion clubs via Road to BAL qualifiers — Petro de Luanda or Al Ahly as clubs, not Angola or Egypt as nations, winner to the Intercontinental Cup vs EuroLeague.
“The BAL is NBA rules.”
Reality: FIBA 4x10, trapezoid key, FIBA goaltending — NBA operates commercial 50% but the court is FIBA, not 4x12 NBA.
“It is a league with home and away.”
Reality: 3 conferences in 3 host cities in March-April, then 8-team single knockout in Kigali in May — 12 to 8 then 3 games to lift.

FAQ

How many teams in the BAL?

12 champion clubs — 3 conferences of 4 in March-April, top 8 to single knockout in Kigali in May, winner to Intercontinental Cup.

Is the BAL NBA or FIBA?

Both — NBA 50% commercial and funding, FIBA sporting rules 4x10, so the court is FIBA not NBA.

What does the BAL winner get?

$400k prize plus qualification to the FIBA Intercontinental Cup vs the EuroLeague and G-League champions in September.
