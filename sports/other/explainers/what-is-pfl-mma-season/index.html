What is the PFL MMA season?

Evergreen • Updated 02 Oct 2026 • 3 min read
Answer: The PFL is MMA's league season, not UFC's matchmake — 6 weight classes, 2 fights regular season with points 3 for win plus 3 KO/sub, 2 decision, 1 draw, top 4 per weight to single-elimination playoffs semis and final for $1m prize, not $508k two-way, with 6 regular 12 over 10 weeks plus 3 playoffs 10 days, not UFC's 12-month matchmake 1 belt.

Explained properly

The PFL season is 6 weights over 10 weeks — 6 weights per night not 1 weight as UFC 1 — regular season 2 fights per fighter: win 3 plus bonus 3 KO 1st, 2 2nd, 1 3rd, so 6 for 1st round KO, 5 for 2nd, 4 for 3rd, 3 for decision, 1 for draw, 0 loss — points 6 is most regular season, so Kayla Harrison's 6 via 1st round armbar per season not UFC's decision 3 — the top 4 per weight on points to playoffs semis single KO with 8 of 30 per weight not 16 of 32 as FIBA World Cup's 32 — the $1m prize final not $508k two-way, which is why Johnny Eblen 2023 PFL 11-0 got $1m vs UFC's $50k bonus for 11-0 with $508k two-way not $1m prize, plus 6 regular 12 not 1 UFC 1 per month.
