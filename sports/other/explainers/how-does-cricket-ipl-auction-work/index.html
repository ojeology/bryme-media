How does the IPL auction work?

Evergreen • Updated 02 Oct 2026 • 3 min read
Answer: The IPL auction is the February player draft where 10 franchises bid with ₹120cr ($14.5m) purse for 25 players, with £m bids like Mitchell Starc ₹24.75cr, 4 retained plus auction, 4 foreign max per XI, salary cap 120cr plus uncapped Indians, right-to-match and accelerated after 100+ pool.

Explained properly

The IPL auction is not the draft order with 96 of 1,001 combinations — it is English auction with paddle — base price ₹20 lakh to ₹2cr, increments ₹20 lakh, hammer after 3 calls, with ₹120cr purse per team (about $14.5m), 25 squad max, 18-25 squad size, 4 foreign per XI mandatory, 4 retained October plus 6 via RTM right-to-match now 2025 with 1 RTM, not 5 retain — the 2024 auction had 331 pool, 72 sold, purse left 15cr for unsold not bought.

The retention vs auction explains the myth. Retain 4 max October at ₹16cr-12cr-8cr-6cr =42cr purse spent, the rest ₹78cr for 21 players at auction — the 4 retain leaves ₹78cr to bid vs 0 retain leaves ₹120cr full, which is why KKR retained 4 at 42cr then bought Starc 24.75cr with 78cr left (32% on 1 player), which is why the 4 retain is 42cr flat not per team bid, so CSK retain 2 at 28cr leave 92cr for auction's 7 picks, not 4.

The foreign cap is 8 per squad but 4 per XI — 8 overseas squad max, 4 per XI plus substitute as impact player now — the 4 per XI means CSK's 8th overseas sits while 4 play, which is why Starc 24.75cr Australian pace plays while Hazlewood sits, and why the auction's 331 pool had 77 overseas sold — the 4 per XI not 11 forces the ₹2cr Indian uncapped vs ₹20 lakh base for overseas 1 cr, so value is the 4-per-XI, not the 8 squad.

The hammer decides more than the base — the 2024 auction Starc 24.75cr was 20cr base plus 4.75cr increments of 20 lakh x 24 increments with hammer at 24.75 after Delhi cap 24cr plus KKR 24.75 final — the 24.75 not 25 flat shows the 20 lakh increment from 20cr base to 24.75's 4.75 extra is 24 increments, which is why the auction's accelerated after 100 not sold plus unsold list at 170 players remaining is the TV's 8-hour show with 72 sold, not all 331.

The evergreen is ₹120cr not £. The purse is ₹120cr cap plus salary cap 14.5m USD, the sold 2024 72 cost 230cr total, 72* average 3.2cr vs NBA's 15-man 168m, which is why the IPL's 25 squad at ₹120cr is 4.8cr per 25 vs NBA 15 at $11m per 15, and why the Impact Player now adds 12th per match but not per XI, so the auction's 25 squad for 14 matches with 7 home plus substitutes.

Picture this: February Bangalore auction: 10 teams ₹120cr purse — KKR retain 4 at 42cr, purse left 78cr, bid Starc ₹24.75cr Australian fast (20cr base plus 24 increments of 20 lakh), squad 25 max 8 overseas but 4 per XI + Impact — 331 pool 72 sold 8-hour accelerated after 100 — the ₹120cr cap that retained 4 leaves 78cr, not 120, the paddle auction not 14-ball lottery's 1,001 combos.

Myths vs reality

“IPL auction is like draft.”
Reality: English paddle auction with base ₹20 lakh-2cr, 20 lakh increments, ₹120cr purse for 25, 4 retained at 42cr leaves 78cr for auction, not order as NBA's 14 balls.
“11 foreign can play.”
Reality: 8 squad max, 4 per XI + Impact 12 — auction's 77 overseas sold but only 4 per XI play, so Starc 24.75 sits Hazlewood.
“All 331 are sold.”
Reality: 72 sold of 331 pool, 72 cost 230cr, average 3.2cr — accelerated after 100 unsold list 170, purse left 15cr shows half not sold.
