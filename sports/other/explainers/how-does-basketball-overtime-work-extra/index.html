How does basketball overtime really work?

Evergreen • Updated 02 Oct 2026 • 3 min read
Answer: Basketball overtime is 5 minutes per period until one team leads — FIBA and NBA both 5-minute OTs with 2 timeouts each, team fouls reset to 4, and the possession arrow does not decide — the 5-minute OT's 2 timeouts decide more than the 5 minutes, with infinite OTs until a winner, which is why 4OT exists but 2OT is the FIBA World Cup's longest.

Explained properly

Five explains the clock. Overtime is 5:00 on the clock, not 3, with a jump ball to start OT1 then possession arrow after — team fouls reset to 4 for the bonus on the 5th, personal fouls carry, timeouts 2 per OT (NBA 2, FIBA 2), so the OT's 5 minutes is 12% of the 40 or 10% of the 48, but the 2 timeouts per 5 is 1 per 2.5 vs regular's 1 per 5, which is why OT is timeout-heavy and the last 2:00 has 4 timeouts plus challenges.

The arrow matters. FIBA OT1 jump ball then arrow, OT2 arrow continues, so the team that won the initial jump does not get OT2 automatically — the arrow's alternate means OT2 goes to the other, which is why AfroBasket's 2OT game in 2021 Angola vs Cape Verde used arrow for OT2 while NBA's 2OT uses jump for OT1 only then arrow for OT2? Actually NBA OT1 jump, OT2 jump? NBA jumps every OT — FIBA arrows after OT1 — the evergreen is arrow vs jump: FIBA arrows OT2, NBA jumps each OT.

Why OT is 5 matters is gambling. The 5-minute 12% extends the 40 to 45 for fantasy points 5 minutes extra with 2 timeouts, which is why the NBA's longest is 6OT 78 minutes at Syracuse vs Rochester 1951, FIBA's 2OT at 50 minutes in 2023 World Cup USA vs Canada 3rd place — the OT's 5-minute period can repeat infinitely, not one like football's 30, so the 4OT exists because 5+5+5+5 never ends until lead, unlike football's extra-time one 30 not 5 infinite.

The scoring in OT is about fouls — team fouls reset to 4, so the 1st foul is not bonus, the 5th is two free throws — the 5th team foul in OT is automatic 2 shots, which is why OT's strategy is to foul early for the bonus, not late as in regulation's 5th, and why the 2OT's 10 minutes total fouls 8 vs 5 in regulation, so the FT rate doubles.

The myth is OT is sudden death. It is 5 minutes full — the 98-98 at 40 becomes 108-105 after OT, not first basket wins, which is why the final 5.0 seconds with jump ball at OT start is 2 possessions, not 1, and why the 3x3 OT is first to 2, not 5 minutes — 3x3 OT is first to 2 points after 21-21, the only sudden 2, while 5x5 OT is 5 minutes full.

Picture this: AfroBasket OT 2021: Angola 75-75 at 40, OT 5:00 — jump to start OT1, Angola wins 84-80, team fouls reset to 4, 2 timeouts each, not sudden death — the 5-minute OT's 2 timeouts decide vs football's 30 extra once, which is why 2023 World Cup USA-Canada went 110-104 in 5 OT 1-0, not 2OT's 5+5 full until lead.

Myths vs reality

“Overtime is sudden death.”
Reality: 5:00 full per OT until one leads — 98-98 becomes 108-105 after 5, not first basket — 3x3 OT first to 2 is the only sudden.
“Fouls carry over.”
Reality: Team fouls reset to 4 on 5th bonus, personals carry, timeouts 2 per OT — so FT rate doubles in OT vs regulation.
“Arrow decides every OT.”
Reality: FIBA: jump OT1 then arrow for OT2, NBA jumps each OT — so OT2 possession flips, not repeats.

FAQ

How long is basketball OT?

5 minutes per period until one leads — 2 timeouts each OT, team fouls reset to 4, not sudden death.

Does OT use jump or arrow?

FIBA jump OT1 then arrow OT2, NBA jump each OT — so OT2 possession flips in FIBA.

How many OTs can there be?

Infinite 5-minute OTs until lead — NBA record 6OT 78 minutes, FIBA World Cup 2OT max recent.
