What are constructors vs drivers championships in F1?

Evergreen • Updated 02 Oct 2026 • 3 min read
Answer: Drivers' is one driver's sum of 25-1 points over 24 GPs, constructors' is team's sum of both drivers' points over 24, with $1bn prize split 14-10% to top team, not the drivers' trophy, with Red Bull's 1st+2nd 25+18=43 per GP vs Williams 11th 1 point, so constructor shows car 70% not driver 30%.

Explained properly

Constructors vs drivers: drivers' championship is driver's 24-race sum 25-1 to 10th plus fastest if top10 — Verstappen's 575 2023 with 19 wins vs Pérez 285 was drivers' 1 vs 2, constructors' is team's sum Verstappen 575 plus Pérez 285 =860 for Red Bull 1st vs Mercedes 2nd 409 (Hamilton 234+Russell 175) =860 vs 409, so Red Bull's constructors 860 not Verstappen alone 575 shows team 70% car plus 2-driver sum vs driver 30% individual — the constructors' 860 prize 14% $140m vs Williams 10th 28 points $15m with cost cap 135m shows car, not pay driver, wins constructors not drivers.

The prize split myth: drivers' trophy is Drivers' (Fangio) trophy with driver's name, constructors' Cup with team trophy $1bn split Pro Rata 14% for 1st (140m), 10% 2nd, 7% 3rd... not equal — the 14% not 5% shows team's $140m vs 9th's $35m with wind-tunnel handicap last 115% vs first 70% aero hours, not equal car, which is why Williams 10th with 28 points prize 35m plus merch 10 not 140 still needs $135m cap gap with owner subsidy vs Red Bull's 860 prize sufficient.

The grid's evergreen myth is drivers equal cars. Drivers not equal as car decides 70% pace — Red Bull's 1-2 43 per GP vs Haas 0 shows car not driver, which is why Pérez 2nd in same car vs Hamilton 3rd in Mercedes 2nd — the same car 1 vs 2 gap 290 points Verstappen-Pérez 290 not 0 shows driver 30% within team, but between teams car's 70% decides 860 vs 409 gap, not Hamilton's 234 beating Pérez's 285? Hamilton 3rd but Mercedes 2nd with Russell sum 409 shows team sum not driver best.

Picture this: 2023: Verstappen 575 drivers' 1, Pérez 285 2, Hamilton 234 3 — Red Bull constructors 860 (575+285), Mercedes 409 (234+175), Ferrari 406 — constructors 860 prize $140m 14% vs Williams 28 10th $35m — constructors is team's 2-driver sum over 24, drivers' one sum, with 70% car vs 30% driver, wind tunnel last 115% vs first 70% not prize alone.

Myths vs reality

“Drivers win constructors.”
Reality: Constructors is team's 2-driver sum over 24 — Red Bull 860 (575+285) vs Mercedes 409, drivers' 575 vs 285 separate.
“Prize equal.”
Reality: Prize 14% for 1st 140m vs 10% 2nd vs 2.5% 10th 25m — Red Bull 140m vs Williams 15m shows 70% car gap.
“Best driver in best car always.”
Reality: Pérez 2nd in best car gap 290 to Verstappen same car shows driver 30% within team, car 70% between teams.
