How does rugby sevens actually work?

Evergreen • Updated 02 Oct 2026 • 3 min read
Answer: Rugby sevens is 7-a-side, 7 minutes per half (14 total), same pitch as 15s but 7 not 15, 5-point tries, no lineout? Actually lineout 2-man, scrum 3-man, with Olympic 12 teams over 3 days and World Series 8 stops — the fastest team sport where 14 minutes is the final, not 80.

Explained properly

Sevens is 7 per side over 14 minutes — 2x7 with 2-minute halftime, 3 subs, sin bin 2 minutes not 10 — space is 7 of 15 on 100m so gaps 10m vs 2m in 15s, which is why the centre's speed 9m/s beats the lock's 7m/s — the sevens' try 5 points plus 2 conversion same as 15s, but conversions are drop-kick not place, and scrums are 3 not 8 with hooker 1 lock 1 prop, lineout is 2 not 8 with jumper 1 lifter 1, which is why sevens looks like sevens not 15-shrink.

The World Series explains season. The HSBC SVNS 2024 has 8 stops: Dubai, Cape Town, Perth, Vancouver, Los Angeles, Hong Kong, Singapore, Madrid final — 12 men's teams 12 women's, each stop 3 pools of 4, top 8 to quarters single KO — the Series winner is points per stop, not one final, which is why Argentina men 2023-24 won Series with 5 stop wins plus 2 finals, while NZ women 6 titles, not a single World Cup like 15s' 7 weeks, so the Series is the 8-stop 12 not the 20 of 15s World Cup.

Olympic 12 is smaller. Olympic sevens is 12 teams over 3 days — 3 pools of 4, top 8 to quarters, single KO — Fiji men gold 2016 and 2020, NZ women gold 2020 — the 12-team 3-day 14-minute format from 7's small field makes the 12 vs 15s 20 vs 12 again, which is why Kenya 7s Shujaa qualify via Africa 1 vs SA — the Olympic 12's 3-pool 4 not 15s 4-pool 5, so the 12's pool is 3 games not 4, with 8-team KO not 20-16 like 15s second round.

The evergreen myth is sevens uses 15 laws. Scrum 3 not 8, lineout throw straight not 5m, kick to touch restarts with tap not lineout? Actually kick to touch from the 22 restarts lineout still — the 14 vs 80 means 80 not 5 points total 5 vs 26, which is why the sevens final is 26-12 in 14, not 22-17 in 80, and why the yellow is 2 min not 10 — 2 min is 14% of 14 vs 12.5% of 80, so proportionate, not light.

The 7's speed bottleneck is not the clock but the 3. The 3:30 substitution window with rolling subs 3 plus 2 by 2024 SVNS with 2 impact — the 7's speed 9m/s vs 15's collision 10m/s with 8-man packs — the sevens' 14 vs 80's 6 games in 3 days Olympic uses day 1 pool 3 games 14 each with 2-hour recovery, which is why the final's 14 second half at 12:00 day 3 is day 3rd game, not 5th as 15s quarter week 5.

Picture this: SVNS 2024 Hong Kong: Fiji men beat France 22-12 final 14 minutes 2x7, Argentina win overall Series with 8 stops Dubai-Madrid — Olympic Paris 2024: NZ women beat Canada 19-12 over 14 — 7 per side 14 minutes 5 plus 2 tries, scrum 3 lineout 2 vs 15s 8, World Series 12 over 8 stops pools of 4 top 8 to quarters, Olympic 12 pools 3x4 top 8 to quarters — the 14-minute 7 not 80's 15 over 3 days.

Myths vs reality

“Sevens is 15s with 7.”
Reality: 14 minutes 2x7 plus 2-min halftime, scrum 3 not 8, lineout 2 not 8, sin 2 min not 10 — same pitch 7 on 100m 10m gaps vs 2m.
“Olympic is 20 teams.”
Reality: 12 teams 3 pools of 4 over 3 days top 8 to quarters single KO — Fiji double gold, NZ women, 14-minute 7, not 80's 20.
“Season is one World Cup.”
Reality: World Series 8 stops Dubai to Madrid final — points per stop, top after 8 wins Series, not one 7-week cup.

FAQ

How long is sevens?

14 minutes — 2x7 with 2-minute halftime, 5-point tries, scrum 3, lineout 2, sin 2 minutes.

How many teams Olympic sevens?

12 — 3 pools of 4 over 3 days, top 8 to quarters single KO — Fiji men double gold.

What is World Series sevens?

HSBC SVNS 8 stops Dubai-Madrid — 12 men's and women's, pools of 4 top 8 to quarters, points per stop.
