What is the T20 World Cup?

Evergreen • Updated 02 Oct 2026 • 3 min read
Answer: The T20 World Cup is 20 teams every 2 years over 28 days — 20 play 4 groups of 5, top 2 to Super 8, top 4 to semis, single 20-over 3-hour games, winner plays 9 games not 50 overs, with the 2026 India defence the 20-team 9-game path that the ODIs 10-team 9-game mirrors in time not overs.

Explained properly

The T20 World Cup is 20 since 2024 expanded from 16 — 4 groups of 5 over 12 days, top 2 per group to Super 8, Super 8 as 2 groups of 4, top 2 per to semis single knockout — the 20's 4x5 means 4 group games per team, not 9 as ODI's 10 single round-robin, which is why Uganda and USA made 2024 as the 20's Associates 8 of 20, while ODI's 10 has 0 Associates via qualifier except Netherlands — the 20 gives 8 Associate spots, the 10 gives 2.

The Super 8 explains the stage. After 4x5, the 8 carry over seeding not points — 8 to 2 groups of 4 play 3 each, top 2 to semis — the Super 8 is 3 games not 4, so the 2024 Super 8 Group 1 had India, Australia, Afghanistan, Bangladesh, each 3 — the 8's 3 decides the semi with one surprise (Afghanistan over Australia), which is why the T20 World Cup's Super 8 is the ODI's top 4 semis' twin with 8 not 4, longer but still single KO after.

The 20-over shapes the 3 hours. Twenty overs per side, 120 balls per innings, 6 overs powerplay 2 outside then 14 with 5, innings 90 minutes plus 20 interval, game 3:30, not ODI's 8 hours or Test's 30 hours — the T20's 120 balls par 170 means 8 per over vs ODI's 320 at 6.4 per over, which is why the T20's 6 vs 14 powerplay's 55 in 6 is 30% of innings, not 20% as ODI's 10, and why IPL's 74 games in 20 overs feeds the T20 World Cup's 20-over, not 50.

The evergreen is 2024 co-host. USA and West Indies co-host 2024 with USA beating Pakistan in group, which is why the 20's co-host's 2 games at 6am India prime-time matter as the 20's gate for Associates — the associate 8 of 20 got Dallas's 6am game because India's prime was Dallas midnight, which is why the 20's Dallas 2024 surface issue was the 6am start for India, not for Dallas, like AFCON's 14:00 for Europe vs 17:00 for Africa.

The qualifier feeds the 20. The 20 qualify via regionals: Europe 2, Africa 2, Americas 1, Asia 3, plus auto 12 — the 12 auto plus 8 via regionals means Kenya can qualify via Africa 2 as 2-1 over Zimbabwe, not ranking alone — the 20's 12 auto is Super 8 plus host, not just ranking, which is why 2026 hosts India and Sri Lanka auto plus 2024 Super 8 eight plus 10 via regionals =20.

Picture this: 2024 USA/West Indies: 20 teams 4x5 — India beat South Africa in Barbados final — super 8: India, Australia, Afghanistan, Bangladesh 3 each, top 2 semis, 9 games per winner 4 group plus 3 super 8 plus 2 KO — 20 overs 120 balls 3:30, powerplay 6 with 2 outside then 14 with 5 — the 20-team 29-day T20 vs ODI's 10-team 47-day 50-over twin.

Myths vs reality

“T20 World Cup is 16 teams.”
Reality: 20 teams since 2024 — 4 groups of 5, top 2 to Super 8 (2x4), then 4 to semis — 9 games max, not 4.
“T20 is 50 over.”
Reality: 20-over 120 balls per innings, 6 powerplay with 2 outside then 14 with 5 — par 170 at 8 per over, not 320 at 6.4.
“Super 8 is 4 games.”
Reality: 3 per team — 2 groups of 4, top 2 to semis — Afghanistan over Australia in Super 8 shows the 3-game gate.

FAQ

How many teams at T20 World Cup?

20 — 4 groups of 5, top 2 to Super 8, Super 8 2x4 top 2 to semis, 20 overs per side 120 balls.

What is Super 8?

8-team second round as 2 groups of 4 with 3 games each after 4x5 — top 2 per to semis, not points carry from group.

Difference ODI vs T20?

ODI 50 overs 10 teams 47 days 320 par, T20 20 overs 20 teams 29 days 170 par — both about 9 games max but 8 vs 3:30 hours.
