What is the G League and two-way contract?

Evergreen • Updated 02 Oct 2026 • 3 min read
Answer: The G League is the NBA's 31-team minor league over 50 games — every NBA team has a G League affiliate, with two-way contracts that pay $508k for up to 50 NBA days plus G League, not the $1m minimum, with 45-day NBA limit now 50 games, not days, used for BAL scouts and AfroBasket prospects.

Explained properly

The G League is 31 teams vs NBA 30 — Capitanes de Ciudad de México is 31st with no NBA parent, so 29 NBA clubs have a direct affiliate like Westchester Knicks, South Bay Lakers, while Portland is the one without pure — the 31 play 50 games over 5 months, not 82, with the Showcase Cup 4 games plus regular 34 plus playoffs, which is why the G League's 50 is the development 82's half, not the full NBA's parity — the 31st Mexico club shows the geography, not the talent pipeline, is the expansion.

The two-way explains the money. A two-way is 3 per NBA team, $508k for up to 50 NBA games plus G League salary $40k, not the rookie $1m minimum — the two-way can be active for 50 NBA games, not 45 days since 2023, and must be converted to standard by the final day to be playoff-eligible, so the BAL's Al Ahly export to the G League can be two-way in October and play 20 NBA games by January plus G League showcases, which is why the two-way's 50-game cap is the call-up, not the full roster's 15.

The assignment matters. The NBA club can assign its rookie 1st pick to the G League for reps without waivers — the rookie's $8m salary stays NBA while he plays 5 G League games — which is why the lottery's 1st can be G League 5 games in November then NBA 70 after, and why the BAL's import can be NBA two-way then G League starter in the same week without the waiver's 48-hour claim, which is why the G League is the evergreen assignment, not the loan's window.

The evergreen myth is G League is second-rate Europe. The G League Ignite was the draft alternative for 18-year-olds vs college, now closed 2024 — the Ignite's 2023 Scoot Henderson vs Wembanyama was the G League's last Ignite vs college's March Madness, which is why the BAL's 18-year-old can choose G League Ignite (now closed) or Overtime Elite vs AfroBasket's college — the path's $500k Ignite was the alternative to NCAA's nil, not the EuroLeague's $1m for Vezenkov.

The call-up explains BAL. The G League's 10-day contract no longer exists replaced by standard 10-day hardship now — the 10-day is 3 max per season per player — so the AfroBasket qualifier's guard who signs 10-day with Miami in March plays 4 G League games in February then 10 NBA days, which is why the BAL's 2024 MVP Jo Lual-Acuil's path was G League to NBL to BAL, not NBA direct — the 31-team G League is the NBA's bench, not Europe's BAL's ceiling.

Myths vs reality

“G League is like loan.”
Reality: Assignment without waivers — 1st pick can play 5 G League plus NBA 70, with two-way $508k for 50 NBA games, not $1m minimum.
“G League is Europe.”
Reality: 31 vs NBA 30 plus Mexico City — 50 games vs 82, Showcase Cup 4 plus 34, now 50-game two-way not 45 days.
“Anyone can be called up.”
Reality: Two-way 3 per team plus hardship 10-day 3 max per player, plus assignment for rookies — the BAL export needs the 50-game two-way, not open tryout.
