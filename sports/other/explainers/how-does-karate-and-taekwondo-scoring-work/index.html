How do karate and taekwondo scoring work?

Evergreen • Updated 02 Oct 2026 • 3 min read
Answer: Karate kumite is 3 minutes, 3 judges, points yuko 1, waza-ari 2, ippon 3 for controlled technique to head/body plus senshu first to 3 tie-break, vs taekwondo is 3x2 minute rounds with PSS electronic socks scoring 2 for body, 3 for head, 5 for turning head kick, with Gam-jeom deductions not 10-point must as boxing, Olympic 68kg 8 weight classes, not UFC 8.

Explained properly

Karate kumite score yuko 1 (punch body), waza-ari 2 (kick body), ippon 3 (kick head or throw + punch) — 3 minutes, first to 8 wins (senshu advantage at 3-point gap? Actually WKF lead 8) — senshu first unsolicited point wins at tie — 3 judges with flags, not 10-point must as boxing 12x3, with video review coach challenge 1 per bout, not Hawkeye's 3 — taekwondo is 3 rounds x2 minutes with PSS electronic sensors in chest hogu and headgear plus socks with magnets — body kick 2, head 3, turning body 4, turning head 5, punch 1 — gam-jeom 1 point deduction per fence, 5 gam-jeom wins for opponent, so taekwondo's electronic 2-5 vs karate's judge 1-3 shows sport vs art: karate controlled with no knockdown, taekwondo full contact with knockdown TKO on head 3-5.

Head contact explains control. Karate light contact — excessive contact hansoku, 3rd hansoku wins opponent — ippon head kick must stop 5cm before head not full contact, so karate 14-18 age ippon not KO vs taekwondo head 3 with full contact with headgear PSS 3 with KO TKO standing 8 count — the karate 14-18 yuko 1 with no head contact youth vs taekwondo 18 head 3 with TKO, which is why karate 2021 Tokyo Olympic had 8 weight classes 75kg-67-68 etc then removed 2024 Paris, while taekwondo stays 4 weights 58-68-80+.

The evergreen myth is martial arts similar belts. Karate WKF kumite World Cup not 4-belt 17 weights as boxing's WBA WBC IBF WBO 68 belts — karate's world cup 1 every 2 years with 60 nations vs taekwondo World Taekwondo GP 4 per year 32 nations — the belt vs dojo not sanction bodies 4 with 3% fee, so karate's WKF kumite medal not belt, taekwondo's WT not WBO's belt 4 — the ranking is federation points not sanction 15 top mandatory 9 months.

Olympic weights: karate Tokyo 8 (3 men 67-75-75+ plus kata? Actually kata not kumite?), now dropped Paris — taekwondo Paris 8 (4 men 58-68-80-80+ 4 women 49-57-67-67+) with 16 qualifiers each weight 16 not 32, so taekwondo 8 weights 128, karate 0, which is why boxing 4 belts 17 weights 68 not karate's 1 WKF world 1 vs taekwondo's WT 4 weights Olympic 8.

Picture this: Olympic 2024: karate kumite 3 min — yuko 1 waza-ari 2 ippon 3 — taekwondo 3x2 min PSS hogu 2 body 3 head 5 turning head — body kick 2 taekwondo vs yuko 1 karate — WKF 1 vs WT 4 GP — the 1-3 judge vs electronic 2-5 with Gam-jeom vs hansoku, not boxing 10-point must 12x3.

Myths vs reality

“Karate and taekwondo same.”
Reality: Karate kumite 3 min light contact yuko 1 waza2 ippon3 with senshu — taekwondo 3x2 min full contact PSS 2 body 3 head 5 turning, Gam-jeom deductions — not 10-point boxing.
“Head kicks all contact.”
Reality: Karate controlled stop 5cm before head not full — excessive hansoku loses — taekwondo Olympic full head 3 with TKO 8 count.
“Belts like boxing 68.”
Reality: One WKF vs WT world not 4 bodies 68 — karate Olympic 8 then removed Paris 2024, taekwondo 8 weights 16 per weight.

FAQ

How is karate scored?

Kumite 3 min yuko 1 punch body, waza-ari 2 kick body, ippon 3 kick head/throw — first to 8 senshu, flag judges not PSS.

How is taekwondo scored?

3x2 min PSS 2 body 3 head 5 turning head — Gam-jeom deduction 1 per, 5 wins for opponent.

Is karate Olympic now?

Tokyo 2021 8 weights then removed Paris 2024 — taekwondo stays 8 weights 16 per 58-80+.
