What are the boxing belts — WBA, WBC, IBF, WBO?

Evergreen • Updated 02 Oct 2026 • 3 min read
Answer: Boxing belts are 4 sanctioning bodies WBA, WBC, IBF, WBO each with a champion per 17 weight classes, not one belt, with lineal, Ring and undisputed 4-belt vs unified 2-3 belts, mandatories, and the evergreen where Usyk holds 4 heavy and Canelo held 4 at super middle, not one belt as UFC's single belt per weight.

Explained properly

Boxing belts are 4 bodies, 17 weights, 68 champions — WBA (1921 oldest), WBC (1963 green), IBF (1983), WBO (1988) — each weight has WBA regular plus super? Actually WBA has super and regular nonsense — WBA regular plus super per weight gives 5 belts per weight, not 4, which is why heavyweight has Usyk undisputed 4 plus Fury lineal — not lineal belt but Ring magazine's lineal plus 4 sanctioning, so the 4-belt era since 2007 undisputed 4 not 3 as pre-2007 3-belt era.

Undisputed needs 4, unified is 2-3. Undisputed undisputed holds 4 WBA(WBA super), WBC, IBF, WBO at one weight in 4-belt era — Usyk heavy undisputed 2024 after Fury, Crawford at welter 2023, Canelo at super middle 2021 — unified holds 2 of 4 not 4, not undisputed — the WBA regular is not counted for undisputed, so WBA super vs regular's 2 per weight makes regular not — the 4-belt undisputed is the evergreen where 2-belt unification defends vs mandatory WBA's 3.5% purse fee per body vs WBC's 3% with 3% per body means 12% sanction fee for 4 belts, not 3%.

Mandatory vs voluntary explains defence. Each body orders mandatory challenger top 15: WBA 1 is Joshua, WBC 1 Wilder, IBF 1 Parker, WBO 1 Zhang — the 4 bodies each order defence every 9 months, so the undisputed 4 must defend 4 mandatories in 9 months not 12 as UFC's champ may defend 2 of 12 months vs top 15 no mandatory per body — the WBA mandatory 9 months vs WBC 9 vs IBF 9 vs WBO 9 equals unblockable 4 in 9, which is why the 4-belt undisputed typically gets stripped of one for not meeting mandatory WBO's 9 with purse bid 25% challenger not 30% champion's 80% split, not voluntary's 50-50 as UFC's split 45-45 plus show.

The evergreen myth is one belt per weight. Four belts per weight 17 weights 68 champions plus WBA regular's 17 extra plus interim 5 gives 90 belts, not 8 as UFC's 8 men's — heavyweight has Usyk 4 plus Fury lineal Ring, cruiserweight Opetaia IBF plus Ring, so the 4-belt 68 not 8 decides why boxing's 68 champions vs UFC's 8 — each body's $1m fee plus 3% gate means 4% vs UFC's gate 16% to UFC not per body, which is why Canelo's 4-belt undisputed paid 12% sanction 4×3% vs Fury's 1 belt 3% not 12, and why undisputed heavy 4-belt purse bid 80-20 champion vs 45-45 UFC's gate split.

The lineal and Ring plus belts: The Ring belt since 1922 lineal lineage where champion beats champion after vacant via top 2 beating — Fury's lineal via Klitschko 2015 vacated? Actually Fury beat Klitschko 2015 for lineal not belt? Actually lineal heavy Fury from Klitschko then Usyk from Fury 2024 — the Ring's lineal 1 vacancy via top 2 — the 4-belt plus lineal vs Ring is 6 belts per weight not 4, so Usyk lineal plus 4 is the true undisputed 5 not 4, plus IBF regular vs super shows 6.

Picture this: Heavy 2024: Usyk WBA(WBA super) 3% WBC 3% IBF 3% WBO 3% hold 4 = undisputed heavy 4 plus lineal via Fury 2015 plus Ring — Crawford undisputed welter — 4 bodies 17 weights 68 champions vs UFC 8 — mandatories 9 months per body 4 in 9 not UFC's 2 in 12 — the WBA regular vs super 5 per weight, purse bid 80-20 champion vs challenger 12% sanction fee for 4 belts vs UFC 16% to promo, not per body.

Myths vs reality

“Boxing has one belt per weight.”
Reality: 4 bodies WBA WBC IBF WBO 17 weights 68 champions plus WBA regular 17 — undisputed needs 4, unified is 2-3, not one.
“Undisputed is 3 belts.”
Reality: Undisputed is 4 belts WBA WBC IBF WBO since 2007 4-belt era — unified is 2-3, lineal Ring is extra 5th.
“Champion defends when wanted.”
Reality: Each body's mandatory every 9 months with 3% fee — 4 in 9 months not 2 in 12 as UFC — not voluntary 50-50.
