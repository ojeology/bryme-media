How does Super League work?

Evergreen • Updated 02 Oct 2026 • 3 min read
Answer: Super League is 12 teams over 27 rounds plus 6-team playoffs and Grand Final at Old Trafford — 12 play home-and-away 22 plus Magic Round plus 5 loop fixtures =27, top 2 playoffs semis home vs 3-6 eliminators, points 2 for win plus 1 Golden Point extra time not 4 points plus bonus as union's 4 pools 5 with 4 points plus try bonus not Super League 2 plus 1 vs NRL 17 with 24 plus 8 playoffs double.

Myths vs reality

“Super League 10 teams.”
Reality: 12 teams 27 rounds 22 plus Magic 1 plus 4 loop — 12 home-and-away not 17 NRL 17 with 24.
“Points same as union 4.”
Reality: 2 for win plus Golden Point 1 extra in 10-min golden — not 4 plus 1 bonus for 4 tries or losing by 7 as union 4 points.
“Playoffs 8.”
Reality: 6-team playoffs 6 of 12 — top 2 semis home vs 3 vs 6 eliminators — not 8 of 20 union World Cup 8-team single.

FAQ

How many teams Super League?

Points in league?

2 for win plus Golden Point 1 extra after 10-min golden — not 4 plus bonus as union.
