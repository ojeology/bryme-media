What is a Formula 1 sprint weekend?

Evergreen • Updated 02 Oct 2026 • 3 min read
Answer: A sprint weekend is a Friday qualify + Saturday sprint + Sunday Grand Prix format over 6 weekends a year — the sprint is 100km (about 30 minutes) with points 8-7-6-5-4-3-2-1 to 8th, no pit stop needed, separate from the Grand Prix's 25-18-15 on Sunday, with sprint qualifying (SQ) setting the sprint grid.

Explained properly

F1 sprint is 6 of 24 weekends — China, Miami, Austria, USA (Austin), Brazil, Qatar 2024 — Friday is single practice plus sprint qualifying (SQ1, SQ2, SQ3) for Saturday sprint, Saturday sprint 100km about 30 minutes plus Grand Prix qualifying for Sunday GP — the sprint weekend has 2 qualifies (SQ Friday and GP qualifying Saturday) plus sprint Saturday plus GP Sunday, not 1 qualify as normal Friday-Saturday practice plus Saturday qualify plus Sunday GP, which is why the sprint weekend has 2 grids: SQ for sprint, GP qualifying for GP.

Sprint points explain MYR. Sprint top 8 get 8-7-6-5-4-3-2-1, GP top 10 get 25-18-15-12-10-8-6-4-2-1 — sprint 8 for 1st is 32% of GP's 25 — the sprint's 8 vs 25 means a sprint win is not GP win, so Verstappen's 4 sprints 2023 with 4 wins 32 points — total 575 with sprints vs 543 without, which is why the sprint's 15-20 laps with no mandatory pit vs GP's 44-78 with 1-2 stops decides strategy: sprint full attack with one tyre, GP 2-stop managing lilac.

Qualifying split matters. SQ is Friday 15:12:15 with soft only, with SQ1 12 min 5 out, SQ2 10 min 5 out, SQ3 8 min top 10, medium? fact? The sprint's 2024 rules SQ has medium at SQ1 SQ2? Actually sprint SQ may mandate medium — the GP qualifying Saturday is normal soft-hard with Q1 Q2 Q3 18:15:12 with soft — the Friday SQ vs Saturday qualifying grid difference explains why some team qualifies 1st Sunday but 7th sprint, as Leclerc 2023 Baku did.

Tyr's sprint vs GP tyre. Sprint uses one tyre no mandatory stop — most start soft or medium and go to 100km with no pit, GP uses 2 compounds mandatory with pit window 1-2 stops — the sprint's 100km 30 minutes with 15-20 laps in street not pit, which is why sprint at Sprint? Actually Brazil's 100km 24 laps São Paulo 100km 24 laps 30 min with no 2-second pit, while GP 71 laps with 2 stops 1:30 race time 71 laps tyre split soft-medium-hard, not just sprint's one.

The evergreen myth is sprint is the race. Sprint is shorter than the 305km GP — GP 190-305km vs sprint 100km — sprint sets sprint points 8 not GP pole at GP. Since 2024 sprint pole not counted as GP pole — Verstappen 2023 had 7 sprint poles but 32 GP poles counted, not 39 — sprint pole is sprint not GP, which is why the sprint weekend's 2-pole weekend has GP pole for Sunday and SQ pole for Saturday, not the sprint pole for GP.

Picture this: China sprint: Verstappen SQ pole Friday Sprint 100km Sat 8 points, then GP qualifying Sat pole Sunday Grand Prix 25 points — 6 sprint weekends 2024 China Miami Austria Austin Brazil Qatar — sprint 100km 30 min 8-1 points no pit, SQ 15:12:15 vs Q1-3 18:15:12 — the sprint's 8 vs GP's 25 plus sprint pole not counted as GP pole, 2 grids 2 points tables on 6 of 24 weekends.

Myths vs reality

“Sprint is the Grand Prix.”
Reality: Sprint is 100km 30min Sat 8-1 points to 8 with no pit — GP is 305km 25-1 to 10 with 1-2 stops Sunday — separate grids 2 qualifies.
“Sprint pole is GP pole.”
Reality: Since 2024 sprint pole not GP pole — Verstappen 7 sprint poles not counted as GP poles, only Sunday Q is GP pole.
“Sprint is all weekends.”
Reality: 6 of 24 weekends 2024 — sprint weekends have 2 qualifies, normal weekends 1 — sprint 6 with 100km, GP 24 with 305.

FAQ

How long is F1 sprint?

100km about 30 minutes Sat — 8-1 points to 8, no mandatory pit, vs GP 305km 25-1 points Sun with 1-2 stops.

How many sprint weekends?

6 of 24 in 2024 — China Miami Austria Austin Brazil Qatar — Friday SQ plus GP qualifying Sat as 2 grids.

Is sprint pole GP pole?

No since 2024 — sprint pole counts for sprint, Q3 pole counts for Sunday GP.
