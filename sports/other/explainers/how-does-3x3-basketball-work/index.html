How does 3x3 basketball work?

Evergreen • Updated 02 Oct 2026 • 3 min read
Answer: 3x3 is half-court basketball with 3 players per team over 10 minutes or first to 21, 12-second shot clock, 1-point inside the arc and 2 outside, played on an 11x15m half court with no coach and rolling subs, Olympic since Tokyo 2020 with 8 teams.

Explained properly

3x3 is 3 vs 3 on a half court — 11 metres wide, 15 long, one hoop, no half-court line to cross — with 1 bench player, so the game is 3 on court plus 1 sub rolling live every dead ball, with no coach on the bench allowed to talk during play, which is why the 3x3 reads like streetball but the FIBA ranking is as formal as 5x5 — the half court is the evergreen that makes it a sprint, not a set piece.

The clock explains the speed. Ten minutes running with a stop on fouls and a 12-second shot clock, first to 21 wins early even if under 10 minutes — a 21 in 7 minutes is common because the 2 for outside is 2 not 3, and 1 inside is 1 not 2, so the maths is compressed — a 21 is about 13 possessions at 60% inside, which is why the 3x3 is 21 in 10, not 80 in 40, and why the foul bonus is 1 free plus possession after 6 team fouls.

The Olympic path is the ranking. 8 teams qualify: the host plus 3 by FIBA 3x3 federation ranking and 4 via 3 qualifying tournaments — Nigeria men are ranked about 40th by federation points from 3x3 events, so the African gate is the OQT, not the ranking, which is why the 3x3 in Paris 2024 had Serbia men and USA women as 1 seeds via ranking and 3 African nations via OQT wildcard.

The 12-second vs 24-second explains the tactic. The 12-second clock forces a shot in one action — drive or catch-and-shoot — so the pick-and-roll is one screen, not two, and the miss plus offensive rebound resets to 12, not 24, which is why the 3x3 is about rebounding 1 vs 1 under the hoop with the arc, not boxing out 5 vs 5 — the half court's 11m width makes the help defender 3m away, not 5m.

The evergreen myth is 3x3 is 5x5 cut in half. It is its own sport with its own World Cup and World Tour — the ball is size 6 women's ball at men's weight, the arc is 6.75m same as 5x5 but 1 for inside and 2 for outside, so the 3x3 21 is about 11x2 plus 5x1, not 7x3, which is why the 5x5 shooter must relearn the value.

No, 3x3 is the only basketball where a 6th man is no man — the game is 3 starters, 1 sub, 21 or 10 minutes, and the ranking is federation, not star — the USA men without NBA stars can be out of the top 8 while Serbia with 4 street pros is 1st.

Picture this: Paris 2024: Place de la Concorde half court 11x15m — USA women beat Spain 17-16 in the final under 10 minutes, Netherlands men beat France 18-17 — first to 21 not 80, 12-second shot clock, 1 for arc inside 2 for outside, 8 Olympic teams via ranking plus OQT, not the 12 of 5x5 — the half-court 10-minute sprint where Serbia men ranked 1st without NBA names.

Myths vs reality

“3x3 is just half of 5x5.”
Reality: Its own FIBA sport with 12-second clock, 1 inside 2 outside for 21 or 10 minutes on an 11x15m half court — ball is size 6 at men's weight.
“5 players per team.”
Reality: 3 on court plus 1 sub rolling live, no coach voice during play — 4 total, not 12, so the sub is the game.
“It is not Olympic.”
Reality: Olympic since Tokyo 2020 — 8 teams via federation ranking plus 3 Olympic qualifiers, first to 21 or 10 minutes.

FAQ

How long is a 3x3 game?

10 minutes or first to 21 — 1 point inside the arc, 2 outside, 12-second shot clock on an 11x15m half court.

How many players in 3x3?

3 on court plus 1 rolling sub, no coach during play — 4 total, not 12, so every sub is live.

Is 3x3 Olympic?

Yes since Tokyo 2020 — 8 teams, host plus 3 by ranking plus 4 via qualifiers, first to 21.
