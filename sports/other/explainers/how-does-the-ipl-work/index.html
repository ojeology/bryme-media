How does the IPL actually work?

Evergreen • Updated 02 Oct 2026 • 3 min read
Answer: The IPL is 10 franchises, March-May, 74 games — double round-robin then playoffs — with a March auction for 25-man squads, a ₹120 crore ($14m) purse per team, 4 foreign players max per XI, and a $6bn TV deal that makes it cricket's Premier League where a 6-week tournament pays more than a year of Tests.

Explained properly

The IPL is 10 teams — Mumbai, Chennai, Kolkata, Bangalore, Delhi, Rajasthan, Hyderabad, Punjab, Gujarat, Lucknow — each plays 14 league games home and away, then the top 4 go to playoffs — 1st vs 2nd in Qualifier 1 winner to final, loser to Qualifier 2 vs winner of Eliminator 3rd vs 4th — so the league is 70 games plus 4 playoffs over about 8 weeks, with every game at 19:30 IST for TV, which is why a season has 74 games, not 38 like football.

The auction explains the evergreen question. Squads are 25 — 8 foreign max in squad, 4 per XI — bought at a March auction with a purse of ₹120 crore per team, where the purse left after retentions is the budget, and the lowest base is ₹20 lakh for uncapped. Mitchell Starc went for ₹24.75 crore ($2.9m) in 2024 as the most expensive, while an emerging player goes for ₹20 lakh — the purse, not the wage, is the cap that keeps the league balanced, though retained stars keep a separate pre-auction fee.

Why 4 foreign per XI is the rule is the Indian core. The IPL is the BCCI's domestic league, so 7 of 11 must be Indian, which develops the national team — the same XI that wins the IPL often has 4 Indian bats and 3 Indian bowlers, which is why the IPL is the feeder for India's T20 World Cup squad, not a foreign exhibition, and why an Indian uncapped can become a star in one season.

The TV deal explains the money. The 2023-27 rights were $6.2bn — $1.2bn per year — split 50% to franchises and 50% to the BCCI, so each franchise gets about $50-60m a year before a ball, which is why the purse can be $14m and still profit — the gate is 30,000 at 7:30pm on a weekday, but the TV is 500m viewers, which is the inverse of the football finance that gates first support.

Format vs internationals shows the calendar. The IPL's 8 weeks in March-May clash with England's County, Australia's Sheffield Shield and West Indies' Tests, so boards release their stars for the IPL because the IPL fee for 6 weeks can equal a year's central contract — Jos Buttler's IPL $1m for 14 games vs England's $1.2m for 12 months — which is why the IPL window is now protected and no internationals are scheduled in it.

Picture this: March auction: Punjab Kings bid ₹24.75 crore for Starc, Gujarat retain Shubman Gill for ₹16 crore pre-auction, then in May Gujarat vs Mumbai at Narendra Modi Stadium with 100,000 — Mumbai post 218 in 20 overs with 12 sixes, Gujarat chase 219 with Gill 104 off 55, win by 3 wickets — 74 games later the final is in Chennai with 2.5 crore viewers per ball, the IPL's 8-week 74-game sprint that pays more than a 5-day Test series.

Myths vs reality

“IPL is an international tournament.”
Reality: A domestic franchise league of 10 Indian cities with 4 foreign per XI — 7 of 11 must be Indian, which is why it feeds the Indian national T20 side.
“IPL teams just buy anyone.”
Reality: Purse is ₹120 crore ($14m) per team, 25-man squad, 8 foreign max in squad, 4 per XI — the auction and the Indian quota keep the 10 balanced.
“IPL clashes with Tests randomly.”
Reality: The BCCI's March-May window is now protected — boards release stars because the IPL fee for 6 weeks can equal a year's national contract.

FAQ

How many teams in the IPL?

10 — 14 league games each plus 4 playoffs over 8 weeks in March-May, 74 games, 4 foreign per XI, 7 Indian.

How are players bought?

March auction with a ₹120 crore ($14m) purse per team — retained stars pre-auction, 25-man squads, uncapped base ₹20 lakh.

How much is the IPL TV deal?

$6.2bn for 2023-27 — $1.2bn per year — split 50% to franchises and 50% to the BCCI, the richest per-game deal in cricket.
