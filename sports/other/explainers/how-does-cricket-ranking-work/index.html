How does cricket ranking work?

Evergreen • Updated 02 Oct 2026 • 3 min read
Answer: Cricket rankings are 100-point scale updated after each series — Tests, ODIs and T20s each separate, 100 for the best, rating vs ranking plus World Test Championship points 12 per win, not a 52-week roll but a match-by-match gap where Australia over India by 2 points means 2% better.

Explained properly

Cricket rankings are ICC 100-scale per format — Tests, ODIs, T20Is separate with 100 for the top — after each Test or ODI the rating moves about 2 points per match, so India's 120 vs Australia 118 in Tests means India 2% better, not games — the 100 is not 2000 as tennis's 2000, but the 100-scale's 3-point gap can be one Test's 2 points plus one ODI's 1, which is why the ranking moves daily not weekly as tennis's 52-week Monday.

The Test ranking plus WTC contrasts. The ICC Test ranking is 100-scale rating after each Test, but the World Test Championship is table points 12 per win, 4 for draw, 6 for tie, over 2-year cycle 6 series per team, top 2 to Lord's final over 2-year — the ranking is 100 after each Test, the WTC is 0-72 points per cycle, so India can be ranking 1 at 120 but WTC 1 at 58 points and still meet Australia ranking 2 at Lord's.

The evergreen myth is ranking decides qualifiers. The ODI Super League decided 2023 World Cup slots via 10 before, but now ODI ranking 8 plus 2 via qualifier decides — the ranking's 100 decides which 8 auto are in the World Cup 10, not the World Test Championship's 2-year table — the T20 ranking's 100 decides no direct slot but seeds the T20 World Cup's 20 as 12 auto plus 8 via regionals, not ranking's top 20 directly.

Why AfroBasket ranking matters is FIBA vs ICC. The cricket ranking's 100 after each match vs tennis's 52-week expiry vs ATP's 2000 for Slam — the cricket's match-by-move 2 points vs tennis's 52-week drop 2000 at expiration explains why cricket ranking shifts after one T20 upset but tennis's stays for 52 weeks with 0 drop, which is why Nigeria's cricket T20 ranking 40 can jump 6 with 2 wins while ATP's Nj m 40 stays as last year's 360 dropped 52 weeks later, not same week.

Picture this: ICC 2024: Test ranking India 120, Australia 118 — 2-point gap, WTC Lord's final cycle 12 per win 6 series top 2 vs 100-scale — T20I ranking India 275, Australia 268 — the 100-scale per format vs 2000 men's Slam vs ATP 19, updated per match not Monday — the 8 ODI ranking auto for World Cup 10 plus 2 via qualifier vs WTC 2-year 6 series.

Myths vs reality

“Ranking is one table.”
Reality: 3 separate 100-scale tables Tests, ODIs, T20Is updated per match, plus WTC 2-year points 12 per win top 2 to Lord's — not one 2000.
“Ranking decides World Cup 10.”
Reality: ODI ranking 8 auto plus 2 via qualifier for ODIs 10 — the ranking's 8 decides half, not the whole 10.
“One win moves 10 points.”
Reality: About 2 per match on 100 scale vs tennis's 2000 for a Slam — the 2-point gap 120 vs 118 is one Test's move.

FAQ

How many cricket rankings?

3 separate 100-scale: Tests, ODIs, T20Is updated per match, plus WTC 2-year table 12 per win top 2 to Lord's.

What is WTC?

World Test Championship 2-year cycle 6 series per team, 12 per win — India's 120 ranking separate from WTC 58 points.

Does ranking decide World Cup?

ODI ranking 8 auto plus 2 via qualifier for the 10 — not all 10 by ranking.
