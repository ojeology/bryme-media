Myths vs reality

“Single session.”
Reality: Q1 18 20 to 15, Q2 15 to 10, Q3 12 for pole 10 — SQ1-3 12-10-8 for sprint 100km not GP Sunday.
“All qualify.”
Reality: 107% rule 107% of fastest Q1 to start — 1:10 vs 1:15 75 fails 74.9 107% of 70 not qualifies.
“Sprint pole is GP pole.”
Reality: SQ pole Saturday sprint 8-1 vs QP pole Sunday GP 25-1 — sprint 6 of 24 weekends SQ not QP.

FAQ

How many qualifying sessions F1?

Q1 18 min 20 to 15, Q2 15 min 15 to 10, Q3 12 min 10 for pole — total 45 min not 305km race.

What is 107% rule?

107% of fastest Q1 to start — 1:10 (70s) 107% 74.9 — 1:15 75 fails not qualifies.

Sprint qualifying same?

No, SQ1-3 12-10-8 for Saturday sprint 100km 30 min 8-1 — Q1-3 18-15-12 for Sunday GP 305km 25-1.
