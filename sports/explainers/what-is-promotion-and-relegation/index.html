League rules

What is promotion and relegation?

The short answer

At the end of a season, the teams at the bottom of a division are relegated — moved down a level — and the best teams from the division below are promoted in their place. Playoffs often decide the final promotion places.

League football is organised as a pyramid. Each division feeds the one above it: win enough matches over a season and you move up; finish near the bottom and you move down. Relegation is usually the bottom three clubs in a 20-team top division, while the second tier typically promotes two clubs automatically with a playoff tournament deciding the third.

The playoffs add a second season at the end of the season: clubs finishing just outside the automatic places compete in short knockout ties for the last promotion spot. That structure is why a sixth-place finish can still end in promotion, and why the playoff final is often described as the most valuable single match in football.

Relegation is not only decided by wins and losses. Points deductions for financial or regulatory breaches can pull a club into the bottom places, and leagues can also apply licensing requirements that affect who is allowed into which division. The broad principle — performance decides your level — holds across the world, but the numbers and the tiebreak mechanisms are set by each league.

In practice

A club finishes third in the second tier and enters the playoffs instead of going up automatically. Three knockout matches later it wins the final and is promoted, while the side that finished second has already been up for weeks. The league table decided most of the story; the playoffs decided the ending.

Common misunderstandings

  • "Every league promotes and relegates exactly three clubs." The numbers differ by country and division — some promote two, some four, and playoff places vary.
  • "Only results on the pitch decide relegation." Points deductions and licensing decisions can also change who goes down or up.

Quick questions

How many teams go down from the Premier League?

Three clubs are relegated at the end of each season, and they are replaced by three promoted clubs from the Championship.

Can a promoted team refuse promotion?

Occasionally, if licensing or stadium criteria are not met, a club can be denied entry to a higher division — the place passes to another club under the league's rules.
